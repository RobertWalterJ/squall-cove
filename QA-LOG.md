# QA log: Port map (?map=port), branch large-map

Method: read all 19 screenshots in qa/, then checked the code. For positions I ran the real terrain generator (genTerrain, seed "port", CELL 3) in Node and tested every placed building and prop against the height field, against the road polylines (oriented-rectangle overlap), and against each other. Syntax check after the edits: last module script extracted and `node --check` passes. Nothing was run in Chrome, nothing committed or pushed. All edits are in index.html.

## Fixed

1. **Grass growing in roads.** buildRoadMesh masked grass with a rectangle (road width + 1) by 4.5 m, axis-aligned. For an east-west road that is only 4.5 m wide across the road, so Main Street (9 m) kept about 1 m of grass on each edge. Now a square of (width + 2), which is correct for every direction (also the diagonal farm lanes).
2. **Repeating white lines across roads (13 junctions).** Every side road started at the centre of the through road, so its edge lines and centre dashes were painted across the through road, and the through road's edge line ran across each side-road mouth. The old fix was an 11 m asphalt diamond, whose corners also stuck out onto the grass (the dark wedge in qa_08 and qa_10). Replaced by one oriented asphalt patch per junction, drawn above the marking layer, sized to the through road and aligned to the side road. The far-side edge line of the through road is left intact.
3. **Buildings and props standing in the quay lane.** Warehouse (-158,52), office (138,54), silo, workshop, guardhouse (-72,44) and all four containers overlapped the 7 m lane (kerb at z 49.5). The warehouse overlapped it by 4.5 m (visible in qa_03, qa_04, qa_05). Moved south: warehouse z 58, office z 57, tank z 60, silo (84,57), guard (-72,54.5), workshop (-124,55), containers z 53.5 (orange moved to x 72 so it clears the silo).
4. **Houses overlapping each other.** The Main Street south-side houses at x -172, 46 and 124 sat on top of the cross-street houses (cx +/- 14). Those three are now skipped. Also skipped: the Main Street houses at x -190 and 124 that sat on the two farm lanes.
5. **Other building/road overlaps.** Guardhouse (190,-44) stood in Main Street: now (190,-50). Office (-200,-80) overlapped the West farm lane: now (-214,-80). Phone box (8,-32) overlapped the Site B road: now (3,-32). Tyre stack at the quay lip moved back from z 98 to z 93.
6. **Trees through buildings.** Only the quay, town pads and roads cleared trees. put() now hides every tree inside each building's footprint (plus 1.5 m), so hangar, warehouses, farm and camp buildings no longer have trees growing through them.
7. **Whole features standing in the sea.** On this coastline (verified by the terrain test and by qa_13 and qa_16) the following were under water: the Raider camp flag (258,-288) at -8 m, its tents, bunker, barrier, command tents and camo net; the Landing beach flag (250,90) at -3 m; the Mountain lake flag (-40,-245) on the lake bed; the beached rowboats, east fishing hut, east windmill, radio mast, log pile and east silo. Because place() refuses land items below 0.2 m, none of those buildings ever appeared, and each produced a "needs dry land" toast at start-up. Fixed by moving them to land: Raider camp to the east peninsula (305,-32) with a matching flat pad; Landing beach flag to (228,84); lake flag to the south shore (-68,-228); rowboats and hut to the west bank of the east inlet; windmill (262,-204); mast (-172,-212); log pile (-262,-140); east silo (262,-154); east barn (262,-92); beach grass scattered over land only. Every placement in layPortBuildings now silently skips if the ground is below 0.2 m, so a future terrain change cannot spam toasts.
8. **South lighthouse and offshore islets under 16 m of water.** The bay carve overwrote the two islets in the terrain code, so the light at (40,292) stood on the sea floor. The islets are now added after the bay is carved (they come back at about 3 m, as MAP-PLAN says).
9. **Battle cover and sandbags in water.** layCover used place(), which toasts for each piece that lands in water (for example at the Landing beach). It now skips silently below 0.3 m.
10. **Bare sand strip in front of each slip mouth.** The three apron concrete pieces leave a gap exactly where each slip meets the quay (sand patch on the concrete in qa_04). Added a land-only concrete strip across each slip mouth.
11. **Countdown numbers in UI text (against house rule).** "Not ready for N more seconds" (Griffon, gunship, paratroopers, boat landing) and "Reloading. N seconds." (naval guns) are now plain wording. The scripted-day toast no longer lists times ("at 1 min, 2 min...").
12. **Battle state leaking between battles.** battleStop did not clear bombs, grenades, placed C4 (meshes stayed in the scene) or the air-support cooldowns and "offline" flags, so a new battle started with the last one's cooldowns. All reset now; C4 geometry and material are disposed when placed charges go off or are cleared (small leak).
13. **Async race in battleStart.** layCover and layAA are chained on asset promises; stop then start quickly could lay cover and AA twice. Now guarded with a generation counter.
14. **Help card out of date.** It said first person has three tools (1, 2, 3). Now describes 1 to 9, 0, minus, and lists R, C, P, T, B and Y, which were bound but undocumented.
15. Removed a dead no-op loop in battleStart (`for (const bt of boats...) { }`).
