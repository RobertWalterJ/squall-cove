# Squall Cove audit: design and sandbox playability (October 2026)

Method: I read the code and ran it at phone size. I did not browse the web for this round, so the design ideas below come from well known patterns in touch builder games, not from a source I can cite.

## Fixed in v4.5.0 (terrain on touch)

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Tapping Land only made one tiny dab. Painting needed a hidden toggle and the finger covered the brush. | New default on phones: a big Sculpt button. Move the map with one finger, aim the gold ring, hold Sculpt with your thumb. |
| In "Finger paints" mode the brush sat under the fingertip. | The brush now sits 70 px above the finger. |
| No undo. One slip and the island was wrecked. | Undo (12 steps) and Reset. On a phone an Undo button appears beside Sculpt. On a keyboard use Ctrl+Z. |
| Dab was too weak to notice. | Stronger dab. |

## Next, in order of value

1. **Save the world.** Terrain edits, boats and people vanish on reload. Save to the phone (IndexedDB, prefixed `squall-cove-`) with Save, Load and New. Without this, building anything lasting is pointless.
2. **Build preview.** Placing is tap and hope. Show a ghost of the item with a rotate handle and a cancel, and let people place several in a row.
3. **Goals that are optional.** The sandbox has no reason to do anything. Add a small set of scenario cards you can pick or ignore: storm rescue, cargo run, ice breakout, harbour defence. Keep free play as the default.
4. **Right-hand button column.** It still covers the map. Fold the rarely used buttons (Quality, Help, Install) into one menu button.
5. **Camera helpers.** Add Recenter and Follow buttons so a lost player is never stuck looking at empty sea.
6. **Orders.** Selecting then tapping is fine, but add waypoints (tap several points to make a route) and a stop-here marker. Formations for groups of boats.
7. **Clearer feedback.** Show the order line from a unit to its target, and a short label when something cannot be done (too shallow, too heavy, boat full).
8. **Reading support.** The help card is dense. Add a read-aloud button and a shorter first-run tip card.
9. **Weight.** The first load is about 20 MB. Load the fleet and people only when first used, as the big ships already do.
