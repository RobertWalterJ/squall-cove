# Port and mountains: audit (7 Oct 2026, version 2)

Looked at from the air, from the sea, and at street level (shots in the session). The world is 768 m, the island about 600 m.

## Good

- The coastline is organic: headland, beaches, bay, peninsula with a river mouth, a lake in the hills. No more square outline or visible seam.
- The harbour reads: straight quay, two slips, breakwater arms, deep basin, cranes, a clear place for boats and cargo.
- Roads are graded into the ground and drawn as continuous ribbons with markings, no overlapping strips.
- The economy runs: builders finish a stone circle in about three minutes, trucks and forklift deliver, farms produce, deliveries arrive when stone runs low.
- Buildings are solid to people and vehicles route round them.

## Bad (and what to do)

| # | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sparse at street level: bare green lawns between isolated houses, no streets feel | More houses on the cross streets, street trees along every road, hedges and fences, benches, signs |
| 2 | The quay is nearly empty: a crane, a few lamp posts, scattered crates | Container rows (real physics containers, stacked), more cargo, fences round the yards, more lamp posts, boats moored along the quay |
| 3 | Few people visible anywhere | About 50 on land: townsfolk on the streets, dock crews, fishers on the quay, farmers, guards, hikers |
| 4 | South-east beach and the east plain are empty | Beach huts, driftwood, rocks, palms, dune scrub; the invasion beach gets a track and a few props |
| 5 | Forest is thin on the hills | Denser forest on slopes, orchards and hedgerows on the farms |
| 6 | Ground colour is one flat green with odd dark brown blotches | Lower the blotch contrast, add meadow patches, field edges, tracks |
| 7 | Mountains are modest hills, not mountains | Raise them further and add rocky outcrops |
| 8 | Lighting in the test renders is flat (sun shadow off) | Not a map issue: shadows are on in the game |
| 9 | Sea shelf glow around the island is a lighter ring | Cosmetic; seabed colour ramp could be tuned |
| 10 | Vehicles crawl at times (crane truck slow to arrive) | Navigation is on a 12 m land map here; give vehicles a finer grid for this map |
