# AC-130 gunship sound spec

Generated by `sound/gen_ac130.py` (procedural, seeded, licence-free). Verified by `sound/verify_ac130.py`. 54 new stems, 1.4 MB total, all in `audio/` and merged into `audio/manifest.json` (existing entries untouched). All mono 44.1 kHz Ogg Vorbis q4, lazy, so `A.loadStem` them when the first AC-130 spawns.

IMPORTANT honesty note: nothing here has been listened to. Levels, spectra, seams and variant diversity were measured by script only. The owner should audition and say what is thin or harsh; every number below is a starting point.

Family resolution: strip `_NN` as usual (`ac130_howitzer_fire` picks 01..04). Far stems are their own families (`..._far`), sub stems are `..._sub`.

Levels: loudest 400 ms RMS is matched inside each class like `gen_heavy.py` (boom -17.5 dBFS, fire/loop -19.3 to -21.3, far -23.3, sub -20). Peaks are all at or below -2 dBFS (measured worst -2.1). Gain 1.0 for near, 0.7 for far, 0.8 for sub (manifest values). Rate jitter in manifest: 0.94 to 1.06 (props 0.9 to 1.15, subs 0.96 to 1.04).

## 3. 25 mm Vulcan-style minigun

Rate of fire baked into the loop: 66.7 rounds per second (80 pulses in 1.20 s, an integer number so it wraps seamlessly).

| stem | n | dur | type | use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ac130_vulcan_start` | 2 | 1.35 s | one-shot | Spin-up: pulse rate climbs from about 3 per second to 66.7 per second over about 0.95 s, hydraulic motor whine rising. Play on trigger press. Start the loop at about 1.15 s after the start begins with a 0.15 s crossfade (the start's last 0.2 s is already at full rate). |
| `ac130_vulcan_loop` | 2 | 1.20 s | LOOP, bus `veh` | Continuous "brrrt". Seamless by construction (circular placement). Pick one variant per burst and keep it for the burst. Rate 1.0 nominal; do not rate-modulate more than 0.97 to 1.03 or the pulse train turns audibly tonal. |
| `ac130_vulcan_end` | 2 | 1.90 s | one-shot | Spin-down: rate decays over about 0.85 s, motor winds down, echoing ring-out to 1.9 s. Play on trigger release; fade the loop out in 80 to 100 ms as the end starts (the end's first pulses match full rate). |
| `ac130_vulcan_start_far`, `ac130_vulcan_loop_far`, `ac130_vulcan_end_far` | 2 each | 1.95 s / 1.20 s loop / 3.10 s | far set | Lowpassed (centroid about 400 Hz), gain 0.7. Use all three in the same order beyond about 150 m. |

Pitfall: the loop is intentionally the same pulse grid in both variants (cross-correlation 0.65 at small lags), so alternating variants adds little; one variant per burst is correct.

## Recommended AC-130 mix (suggestion)

Prop loop is the continuous bed; howitzer fire is the loudest event (duck the prop 6 dB for 1 s on fire); Bofors is a hammering mid-range stream; Vulcan is the highest and densest (centroid 3 kHz), so it reads as a tearing sound over both. They differ in time structure as well as tone: howitzer is a rare, long, sub-heavy boom with rattle; Bofors is isolated sharp 150 Hz thumps at 2 to 3 Hz; Vulcan is a continuous 67 Hz buzz-rip.

# Addendum 2: punch pass on the per-surface hits

Owner feedback: Vulcan hits needed more punch and depth, and a brighter, shorter squeak. All names are unchanged (files overwritten; the previous versions are backed up in `sound/out_old/`). Regenerated by `sound/gen_ac130_imp.py` (`python gen_ac130_imp.py v` for Vulcan + stitch, `b` for Bofors), measured by `sound/compare_punch.py`. Not auditioned.

Structure of every Vulcan hit now: (1) punch at 0 to about 70 ms, a fast-attack (0.4 ms) 170 to 62 Hz sub drop plus a low-mid slap with a tight 22 ms tail, no low smear; (2) from 8 to 30 ms later the squeak layer: zip sweeps falling from 7.5 to 12.5 kHz, a 5 to 11.5 kHz hiss, and sharp 4 to 12 kHz grit ticks, each element 20 to 80 ms with a very fast decay. Hits shortened to 0.25 to 0.30 s (metal 0.30, others 0.25; person 0.27) so 10 to 25 per second does not mud. Person remains a cartoon thump (no vocal), with a heavier low end and a light slap. The stitch stems (1.15 s) and far stitches were regenerated from the new hits. Bofors hits got a smaller punch (peak sub 150 Hz, tau 30 ms) and an extra bright crack on top; their shrapnel zings were shortened from 120 to 350 ms to 50 to 140 ms.

Measured, old versus new (mean over the 4 variants). low30 = RMS dBFS of the 60 to 200 Hz band in the first 30 ms; rel = low30 minus whole-file RMS (how front-loaded the low end is); sqCen = spectral centroid of everything after 25 ms high-passed at 2.5 kHz (the squeak layer); sqDur = ms after 25 ms until that high band's envelope has fallen 30 dB below its own peak for good.

Notes: low30 is a small improvement for dirt, concrete and person because the old versions already had a thump (3 dB), but the first 30 ms low band now carries 2.8 to 3.3 dB more of the file's energy for Vulcan dirt and concrete, and the old metal and water hits had essentially no punch at all (-55 and -48 dBFS). Vulcan person was already heavy, so it is nearly unchanged (+0.4 dB relative). The Bofors squeak centroid fell slightly (-4 to -7 percent) because the new low punch and crack dominate the whole-tail average and the debris layer was not changed; its zings are shorter but the debris patter, which I left alone, sets sqDur. Peaks are at or below -2.0 dBFS (Vulcan hits -3.2 to -6.5, stitches -7.2 to -8.4, Bofors -2.0 to -6.3); loudest-400 ms RMS is unchanged at about -22.2 (Vulcan hits), -21.2 (stitch), -19.3 (Bofors). Variant cross-correlation now 0.53 to 0.72 for the Vulcan hits (higher than before at 0.05 to 0.53, because the shared punch template dominates), stitch 0.06, stitch far 0.16. If repetition is audible, vary rate 0.9 to 1.1 and gain per hit, and never fire the same variant twice in a row.
