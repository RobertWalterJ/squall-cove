# AC-130 gunship sound spec

Generated by `sound/gen_ac130.py` (procedural, seeded, licence-free). Verified by `sound/verify_ac130.py`. 54 new stems, 1.4 MB total, all in `audio/` and merged into `audio/manifest.json` (existing entries untouched). All mono 44.1 kHz Ogg Vorbis q4, lazy, so `A.loadStem` them when the first AC-130 spawns.

IMPORTANT honesty note: nothing here has been listened to. Levels, spectra, seams and variant diversity were measured by script only. The owner should audition and say what is thin or harsh; every number below is a starting point.

Family resolution: strip `_NN` as usual (`ac130_howitzer_fire` picks 01..04). Far stems are their own families (`..._far`), sub stems are `..._sub`.

Levels: loudest 400 ms RMS is matched inside each class like `gen_heavy.py` (boom -17.5 dBFS, fire/loop -19.3 to -21.3, far -23.3, sub -20). Peaks are all at or below -2 dBFS (measured worst -2.1). Gain 1.0 for near, 0.7 for far, 0.8 for sub (manifest values). Rate jitter in manifest: 0.94 to 1.06 (props 0.9 to 1.15, subs 0.96 to 1.04).

## Recommended AC-130 mix (suggestion)

Prop loop is the continuous bed; howitzer fire is the loudest event (duck the prop 6 dB for 1 s on fire); Bofors is a hammering mid-range stream; Vulcan is the highest and densest (centroid 3 kHz), so it reads as a tearing sound over both. They differ in time structure as well as tone: howitzer is a rare, long, sub-heavy boom with rattle; Bofors is isolated sharp 150 Hz thumps at 2 to 3 Hz; Vulcan is a continuous 67 Hz buzz-rip.
