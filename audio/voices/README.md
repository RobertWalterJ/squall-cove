# Expressive battle voices (Chatterbox roster)

1124 mono 44.1 kHz Ogg q4 files, 17.4 MB in total, each under 60 KB (largest is 31 KB). 11 synthetic voices, each recorded in two delivery styles:

- `shout`: raw, close-range, yelled over battle. Play when the speaker is physically near the player.
- `radio`: band-limited 300 Hz to 3.4 kHz, mild distortion, carrier hiss, squelch click before and after, light compression. Play when the speaker is far (squad member who moved off, commanders, HQ, aircrew).

Files are `audio/voices/<voice>/<voice>__<textslug>__<style>_t<take>.ogg`. `lines.json` maps every file to: text, role, voice, style, take (1 or 2), `vlines_key` (the matching key in `index.html` VLINES, or null for new groups such as commander orders), `group` (our own grouping key), duration_s, kb, peak_db. `voices.json` describes each voice. Nothing here is wired into the game yet.

### Accent caveat (please read)
Every voice identity comes from a SYNTHETIC reference clip made with Kokoro-82M, which only offers US and UK English voices. Chatterbox copies the reference timbre, pace and accent, so the roster covers US and UK accents, male and female, young and older, plus a calm commander and HQ officer. Irish, Australian and South African accents were NOT produced: no synthetic reference with those accents is available from the licensed sources we already own, and we did not clone any real person. If those accents are wanted, add a synthetic reference with that accent (for example a licensed open TTS voice) to `tools/voices/make_refs.py` and run `roster <id>`.

## Quality gate: measured, not heard
Nobody listened to these before they were written (the generator cannot). Choices were made by measurement: duration, release tail, peak, centroid. Every line is generated with Chatterbox, trimmed to the last consonant burst (-26 dB below peak; -14 dB for 'Up' lines), and rejected and regenerated up to 4 times if its duration is implausible (under 0.05 s per letter, which means swallowed words, or babble above 0.35 s + 0.16 s per letter). 42 lines were regenerated by that rule. `tools/voices/verify_voices.py` reports 0 flags (format, size, decoded peak at most -2 dBFS, duration). Expect that some takes still need a human ear: audition at least the squad and enemy shouts before wiring, and delete or regenerate any bad line (delete its files and entries, then rerun `roster <id>`).

Shout finish: presence lift 1.2-4.2 kHz, 110 Hz high-pass, compressor, tanh saturation (drive 2.2), hard clip, 1.12x WSOLA speed-up. Radio chain: compressor then the approved headset chain from `make_samples.py`. For michael, lewis, echo, nicole, daniel, george and adam the radio file is the same take as the shout through the radio chain (they shout into the radio). fable, emma, sarah and fenrir have a separate calmer radio take (exaggeration 0.45 to 0.6).

## Settings
- Model: Chatterbox (Resemble AI), `ResembleAI/chatterbox` from the Hugging Face cache, fp32, CUDA, temperature 0.8.
- Shout: exaggeration 0.8 to 1.05 by voice (combat lines at the voice value, routine acknowledgements -0.15), cfg_weight 0.25 to 0.4 (+0.08 on routine lines).
- Radio takes: exaggeration 0.45 to 0.7, cfg_weight 0.4 to 0.5.
- Seeds are fixed per line, so a rerun is reproducible on the same stack.

## Regenerate
```
tools/voices/.venv/Scripts/python tools/voices/make_refs.py             # synthetic Kokoro reference clips -> tools/voices/work/refs
CB_PY="<python with torch+CUDA and chatterbox-tts>"
"$CB_PY" -P -u tools/voices/make_expressive.py roster [voice ids]       # all voices, resumable (skips existing files)
tools/voices/.venv/Scripts/python tools/voices/verify_voices.py         # measurement gate
```
Run `-P` (no script directory on sys.path). `make_expressive.py` imports torch, librosa, sklearn and transformers in a fixed order before chatterbox; any other order crashed on this machine (WinError 6714 or an access violation in torch._dynamo). Detached launch from a tool shell needs the process kept alive by the parent (run it as a background job and poll the log). `VOX_REDO=1` deletes implausible lines and regenerates only those. Line inventory and voice settings live in `tools/voices/roster_lines.py`.

## Suggested lazy loading (when wired)
Total 17.4 MB is too much to preload. Proposal, by group of files:
1. Preload at battle start: the player's squad voice(s) only: lewis shout + radio (3.1 MB; or take-1 only, 2.2 MB) plus the first lines of the key families contact, taking_fire, man_down, medic_call, grenade. Defer the rest of that voice.
2. On first use: echo and nicole (2.7 MB each) only when a squad member with that voice spawns; daniel (2.6 MB) when a squad leader exists; load take 2 lazily after take 1 has played once.
3. Event-driven: michael (0.4 MB) when a gun station opens; sarah (0.8 MB) at the first medic call; fenrir (1.1 MB) at the first aircraft or medevac; fable and emma (1.4 + 0.7 MB) at the first commander order or objective event; george and adam (1.0 MB each) when enemies are within about 60 m.
4. Radio files are only needed for far speakers: skip them on mobile for voices that are always near (enemy shouts), and skip shout files for commander, HQ and aircrew voices that are only ever on the radio.
5. Vehicle chatter groups (mount, dismount, full, climbing, in_position, bail) only for lewis, daniel and echo/nicole as listed; load on first vehicle use.
