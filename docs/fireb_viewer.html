Fire sprite viewer

Looping flipbooks baked from Blender's Mantaflow gas simulator, plus lifecycle, spread-front and explosion demos. Everything runs in the page with no server and no countdown display.

Stages are curves applied to the loop (scale, brightness, erosion, smoke). The viewer approximates erosion with contrast; in the game a shader does it exactly.

Spread front (grass fire)

Seamless front tiles placed side by side, a leading piece at the advancing end, a trailing piece at the dying end and a smouldering burnt strip behind.

Explosion with flaming debris

One-shot fireball, then debris on ballistic arcs. Each chunk carries a stretched ember streak that follows its velocity and cools; a shock ring and heat haze expand with the blast.
