# Weapons, impacts, movement, foley, battle UI and battle ambience (2026-10)

Generated by `gen_weapons.py` (guns, handling, shells, passes, impacts, UI stings), `gen_weapons_move.py` (steps, jump/land, foley), `gen_weapons_amb.py` (four ambience beds) and the shared `wpn_common.py`. Run `python render_all.py gen_weapons` then `python deploy.py` (the table below is rebuilt by `write_weapons_notes.py`, which reads `notes_weapons_template.md`). Everything is deterministic (seeded from the stem name). Mono 44.1 kHz Ogg Vorbis, quality 4 (beds quality 3). Cartoon-safe: no gore, no screaming, no speech. The only voiced sounds are the short comic "oof" (imp_body), the mild hit breath (foley_breath_hurt) and a distant dog bark in amb_town.

Existing stems (`veh_gun_fire_01..04`, `veh_gun_shell_fly_*`, `veh_mg_*`) are untouched. Single-variant stems have no numeric suffix (`wpn_pistol_far`, `foley_pickup`, `foley_drop_crate`, `foley_heal`, all `ui_*`, the four `amb_*`); multi-variant stems are `_01.._NN`.

## Groups (deploy.py RULES, placed before the generic `amb_` and `ui_` rules)
- `weapons` (lazy): `wpn_*`, `imp_*`, the battle UI stings (`ui_flag_*`, `ui_tickets_low`, `ui_respawn`, `ui_victory`, `ui_defeat`, `ui_hitmarker`, `ui_kill`) and `amb_battle_distant`.
- `people` (lazy): `step_*`, `foley_*`.
- `weather` (lazy): `amb_farm`, `amb_town`, `amb_quay`.
- Every other `amb_*` and `ui_*` stem still goes to `core`. The runtime must load these lazy groups when a battle starts; `index.html` was not touched by this work.

## How to trigger each (pick a variant at random from the family)
- Gunshots: `wpn_<gun>_fire_0N` at the muzzle on fire. Use `wpn_<gun>_far` instead when the listener is beyond roughly 120 m (manifest gain 0.7, maxDist 900). Optionally add `wpn_gun_echo_tail_0N` 80 to 150 ms after a shot on open ground. The rifle and sniper already carry slap-back echoes.
- Handling: `wpn_reload_mag_out` then `wpn_reload_mag_in` for magazine guns (about 0.6 s apart), `wpn_reload_bolt` for bolt actions, `wpn_pump_shotgun` after each shotgun shot, `wpn_dry_click` on an empty trigger pull, `wpn_draw` when equipping, `wpn_knife_swish` on a melee swing.
- Shells: `wpn_shell_<surface>` 0.15 to 0.3 s after a shot at the ejection point. Concrete/asphalt use concrete, grass/dirt/sand/mud use dirt, metal decks and hulls use metal.
- Bullet passes: `wpn_whiz` when a bullet passes within about 3 m of the listener. `wpn_ricochet` when a bullet hits hard metal or stone and deflects.
- Impacts: `imp_<surface>` at the hit point (dirt, concrete, wood, metal, glass, sand, water). `imp_body` when a person takes a hit, `imp_ko` when one is knocked out.
- Footsteps: `step_<surface>_0N`, once per footfall (each walking step already contains the heel and the toe). `step_run_hard` on concrete, asphalt, wood and metal; `step_run_soft` on grass, sand, mud and dirt. `foley_jump` on take-off, `foley_land_hard` or `foley_land_soft` on landing by surface hardness. `step_water` is wading.
- Foley: `foley_cloth` low-level movement filler, `foley_gear` while carrying a rifle and turning or running, `foley_pickup`, `foley_drop_crate`, `foley_swim_stroke` per stroke, `foley_breath_hurt` on a non-fatal hit (together with `imp_body`), `foley_heal` (ui bus) on a heal.
- Battle UI (bus `ui`): `ui_flag_capture` when the player's side takes a flag, `ui_flag_lost` when one is lost, `ui_tickets_low` once when the threshold is crossed (it must not repeat like a timer), `ui_respawn`, `ui_victory` and `ui_defeat` (4 s each), `ui_hitmarker` on a hit given, `ui_kill` on a kill.
- Beds (bus `amb`, loops of 16 s at about -29 to -30 LUFS): `amb_battle_distant` near a front line (the silent gaps are deliberate, the quietest about -55 dB), `amb_farm` over fields, `amb_town` in built-up areas, `amb_quay` at harbour edges. Seams are zero-crossing; crossfade between beds in the runtime.

## What is weak (measured, not heard)
- None of it has been auditioned. Gunshots are layered noise and sine models (click, crack, body, boom, thump, reverb tail, slap-back). Rifle, sniper and pistol are still low-heavy (about 40 to 50 percent of energy under 200 Hz, only 3 to 9 percent above 2 kHz), so on laptop speakers they may sound smaller than on headphones.
- The distant-battle bed re-uses the far shots at low level in a 16 s loop (three fire fights, two booms and one lone shot), so it may sound repetitive over a long listen.
- The dog bark in `amb_town` is a vocal-synth approximation and the likeliest thing to sound wrong. The FM bird chirps in `amb_farm` sound synthetic up close.
- The "oof", the hurt breath and the ricochet "pee-yow" are synthetic. The oof may sound like a robotic vowel; tell me if any of these is unpleasant or unclear.
- Footsteps are a heel strike plus a toe slap. Mud, water and gravel are the least convincing. Asphalt is still thump-heavy (about 70 percent under 200 Hz).
- The brass stings (flag capture, victory, defeat) are sawtooth stacks through a moving filter, not real brass, so the 4 s fanfare and resolve are simple and may feel thin.
- `ui_tickets_low` is a low tense pulse; if it reads as a countdown, say so (the brief forbids timer sounds).
- Mono only. Shells and impacts are short and dry with no per-surface reverb.
- Housekeeping: `gen_weapons*.py` and some `sound/out/*.ogg` files show as tracked-modified in git, so another process has already staged or committed an earlier copy; I did not commit anything.
