# Squall Cove ideas backlog

A living list. Add anything that would let people express a different idea in this sandbox. Newest at the top.

## Done in v4.7
Placeable water (spring, big spring, pour, drain) that flows downhill, pools and drains to the sea. Catastrophes: hurricane with storm surge, tsunami, tornado, lightning storm, earthquake, meteor, and Calm.

## Done in v4.6
Saving and loading, build preview with Turn, optional scenarios, waypoint routes with loop, order lines, Recenter and Follow, read aloud, one Menu button.

## Next candidates
- More scenarios: ice breakout, lighthouse supply, regatta (race around marks), salvage a sunk boat.
- Scenario maker: place things, then save the setup as your own scenario.
- Photo mode: hide the buttons, free camera, save a picture.
- Day-night cycle you can let run on its own.
- Wrecks you can salvage after a sinking, and oil or debris trails.
- Tides.
- Formations for groups of boats.
- Share a cove as a short code or file.
- Sound: engines, gulls, bell buoys, rain on the water.
- Lazy loading of the people models to cut the first download.
- Dams and canals: water already obeys the land, so a lock or a waterwheel would be a natural next toy.
- Boats in lakes (the placement check only knows the sea today).
- Fire that spreads over forest, and rain that feeds springs.
- Flood warnings and rescue scenarios built on the new disasters.
