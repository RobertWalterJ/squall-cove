# Squall Cove ideas backlog

A living list. Add anything that would let people express a different idea in this sandbox. Newest at the top.

## Done in v4.6
Saving and loading, build preview with Turn, optional scenarios, waypoint routes with loop, order lines, Recenter and Follow, read aloud, one Menu button.

## Next candidates

- Photo mode: hide the buttons, free camera, save a picture.
- Day-night cycle you can let run on its own.
- Wrecks you can salvage after a sinking, and oil or debris trails.
- Tides.
- Formations for groups of boats.
- Share a cove as a short code or file.
- Sound: engines, gulls, bell buoys, rain on the water.
- Lazy loading of the people models to cut the first download.
